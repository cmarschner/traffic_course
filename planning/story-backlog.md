## Module 2 (valuing time and access)
- **Jules Dupuit** (1804–1866), Ponts et Chaussées; 1844 "De la mesure de l'utilité des travaux publics" invented consumer surplus.
  Price discrimination in rail fares (Tubaro quoting Dupuit 1849): the companies differentiate comfort to guard against the
  "avarice" of the rich. Mocked the Thames Tunnel as "only a useless curiosity". Portrait `img/people/dupuit.jpg` is tiny (115 px)
  and of weak provenance. The roofless third-class carriage quote is UNVERIFIED; don't use it.
- McFadden BART details: auto-alone predicted 55.8% vs. actual 59.9%; bus 14.9% vs. 12.2% (Nobel lecture pp. 334–335).
