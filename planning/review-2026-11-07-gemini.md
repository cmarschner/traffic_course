# External review, 7 November 2026 (Gemini)

Source: a Gemini answer, pasted by the editor, on the live site. Two questions: is the course
comprehensive, and what is missing?

## Verdict, summarized

Comprehensive because it joins disciplines usually taught apart (traffic engineering, welfare
economics, planning, equity) and compares seven countries instead of one manual. Praised: the
four-part structure, the three layers (In brief, main text, Under the hood), Vision Zero,
Singapore's pricing and quotas, French roundabout capacity models, Japanese simulator
verification, Dutch social cost-benefit analysis. Not a substitute for a local licensing exam
(US PE, German FGSV rules).
